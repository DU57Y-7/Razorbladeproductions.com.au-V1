Boots and Barrel Country Music Festival

Boots and Barrel Country Music Festival is a major live-event project being developed by Razor Blade Productions for Logan, Queensland. The event is being built around live Australian country music, a strong local identity and a full festival experience for artists, partners and patrons.

More event information, artist announcements and ticketing details will be released as the project develops.
