01 / ABOUT

Built from a love of country music.

Razor Blade Productions is a new Logan-based production company built around a simple idea: great country music deserves great production.

We are starting local, learning the industry and building something of our own. From live events and media to production and promotion, Razor Blade Productions is about getting involved, backing artists and helping create memorable experiences.

02 / OUR STORY
01

A father. A son.
A shared passion.

Razor Blade Productions started with a father and son from Logan who shared a love for country music and a desire to be more than just people in the crowd.

After years of enjoying the music, the atmosphere and the people that make the country scene special, Mitchum and Dustin decided it was time to get involved. What began as an idea around the kitchen table became Razor Blade Productions — a small, ambitious company with its sights set on making a mark in the Australian industry.

We're at the beginning. And that's exactly where we want to be.
