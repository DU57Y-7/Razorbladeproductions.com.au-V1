01 / The Team

Mitchum McGuire

Director

Mitchum is a co-founder and Director of Razor Blade Productions, helping shape the company’s direction and lead the planning of its live event projects. With strong ties to Logan and a long-standing passion for country music, he focuses on turning big ideas into practical, well-run experiences for artists, partners and audiences.

Dustin McGuire

Media & Marketing Manager

Dustin is a co-founder and the Media & Marketing Manager at Razor Blade Productions. He brings the brand to life across digital content, promotion and audience engagement, helping build the identity of each project and connect Razor Blade Productions with the country music community.
